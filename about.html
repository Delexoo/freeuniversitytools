About

Built by a student, for students

One place for free study tools — no ads, no clutter, hand-picked listings.

What is this?

I’m a student. I got tired of jumping between bookmarks and random blogs to find free study tools and AI stuff, so I built this: one place, no ads, no clutter.

Everything here is hand-picked. If it’s on the list, it’s useful and free (or has a solid free tier). The goal: help you study smarter and spend less time searching.

Maintained as a side project. New tools get added when I find something worth sharing.

Report a problem

Found misinformation, a broken link, or something that shouldn’t be listed? Message me on Telegram, @delexoo, and I’ll fix it.
